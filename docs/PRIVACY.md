# Vyrup Privacy Policy

Vyrup is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. 

## Data Storage
- **Offline First:** All your financial data, transactions, and categories are stored locally on your device. 
- **No Accounts & No Cloud:** Vyrup does not require an account, does not offer cloud sync, and does not upload your data to any external servers. The app literally does not have the Android `INTERNET` permission.

## CSV Data Export
- Any data you export as a CSV belongs entirely to you. You are in full control of where you share or save it using the standard Android system file picker.

## Voice Recognition
- Vyrup uses Android's built-in `RecognizerIntent` to convert speech to text. 
- **Important:** Depending on your device settings, this system service may send audio to Google's servers for processing. Vyrup only receives the transcribed text draft from the system.

## Analytics & Crash Reporting
- Vyrup does not include any third-party analytics trackers (like Firebase Analytics) or crash reporting SDKs that upload your data.

By using Vyrup, you retain 100% ownership and privacy over your personal finance data.
